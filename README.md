## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Multilingual Voice & Touch Patient Intake
- **Conversational Interface**: Intuitive 4-step intake for patients to narrate symptoms via voice or touch.
- **Multilingual Support**: Real-time language translation for vernacular patient languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, etc.) into structured clinical summaries.
- **Accessibility First**: Designed for both assisted kiosk entry and independent smartphone submission.

### 2. Intelligent Document & Paper Scribe
- **Prescription & Lab OCR**: Digitizes handwritten prescriptions, discharge summaries, and lab reports into structured temporal medical data.
- **Timeline Synthesis**: Eliminates repetitive questioning by presenting clinicians with a unified, chronological patient timeline.

### 3. Clinician OPD Command Intelligence Dashboard
- **Triage & Queue Prioritization**: Color-coded acuity indicators (Emergency, Priority, Standard) based on vital trends and red-flag symptoms.
- **EHR & AYUSH Integration**: Harmonized clinical intelligence supporting allopathic and AYUSH clinical pathways.
- **Chief Complaint & Clinical Summary**: Generates succinct clinical briefs with suggested opening consultation questions.

### 4. Patient Case Sheet & Longitudinal Records
- **Standardized Medical Summaries**: FHIR/ABDM-compatible case sheet layout with symptom graphs, vitals, allergies, and medication history.
- **Interactive Print & Export**: Ready-to-print or export case sheets for hospital information systems.

### 5. OPD Analytics & Hospital Flow Intelligence
- **Operational Metrics**: Real-time tracking of patient throughput, average wait times, triage distribution, and kiosk completion rates.
- **Resource Allocation**: Departmental load heatmaps to help hospital management optimize staff deployment.

## 🔒 Privacy, Security & Compliance

DocSee is built with privacy-by-design principles:
- **Zero Raw Audio Retention**: Voice streams are transcribed ephemerally and discarded after structured extraction.
- **Patient Verification**: Patient reviews and confirms their summary before it is shared with the OPD care team.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Secure access isolation between kiosk terminals, triage nurses, and attending physicians.
- **ABDM / FHIR Compliance**: Aligned with Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission standards.
